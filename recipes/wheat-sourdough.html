wheat sourdough

White + 25% whole wheat on lievito madre, a stiff 43% starter — my standard, super-active one, the starter I baked bread and pastry on at my bakery. Different starter? Don't worry, the recipe adapts. Dough hydration 75%. Sounds scary, it's simple.

what goes in

Starter 43% = 21% of the flour. Inside it: 50 g flour + 21 g water (600 g loaf), 96 g flour + 41 g water (1150 g loaf).

liquid starter 100%?

  1. aSwap so the flour and water in the dough stay the same.
  2. b600 g: starter 100 g (not 71), water 204 g (not 233). Everything else the same.
  3. c1150 g: starter 192 g (not 137), water 391 g (not 446). Everything else the same.
  4. dAny other starter: it must carry 50 g / 96 g of flour; cut the dough water by whatever it brings above 21 g / 41 g. Or let the calculator do it.

0326°. the number nobody checks
  1. aFirst fold, then measure the dough temperature.
  2. b26 °C or below. Above it fermentation runs ahead and the dough gets weak.
  3. cMine was 27. Oops. First 30 min in the fridge — fixed.
  4. dToo warm is not a disaster, you cool it down. Next time — mix with cold water.

▶ step 3 / 6
04folds & waiting
  1. aA fold every 30 minutes, four in total.
  2. bBy the third one you see bubbles. A good sign.
  3. cOn the fourth, check the gluten: stretch a piece thin. A window like this is what we want.
  4. dNow wait until the dough grows by 50–60%. Mine took about an hour and a half. And a sunset — sorry for the light.

timing at a glancewatch the dough, not the clock
  1. aAutolyse 30–50 min → salt, knead
  2. b4 folds, every 30 min → bulk until +50–60%
  3. cPreshape, rest 20–30 min → shape, proof 20–30 min → fridge overnight (~7 °C)
  4. dBake 250 °C lid on 20 min + 220 °C lid off 20–25 min

Timing is approximate. A liquid starter may run a little faster or slower than lievito madre.
