how the math works

  1. aFlour is 100%. Water, salt and starter are percentages of flour, not of the dough.
  2. bThe starter brings flour and water with it. The calculator subtracts both, so the final hydration stays at 75% whichever starter you use.
  3. cPrefermented flour — the flour inside the starter — is 14.7% of all flour. That is why stiff and liquid give different starter grams for the same loaf.
  4. dDough weight = flour × (1 + hydration + salt). For this loaf: flour × 1.77.
×1.77flour → dough

So 339 g of flour makes a 600 g loaf, and 650 g makes 1150 g. Your bowl is the only limit.
